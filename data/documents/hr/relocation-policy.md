# Relocation Policy

*Owner: People & Culture · For managers and HR*

This policy applies to internal moves between Milan and Lisbon and to new hires relocating from another country.

## Relocation package

| Level | Lump sum |
|---|---|
| L1–L3 | €5,000 |
| L4–L5 | €8,000 |

In addition, Quillmoor covers:

- **Temporary housing** for up to **30 days**
- One **house-hunting trip** of up to **3 days**
- **Language lessons** (Italian or Portuguese) up to **€1,000**

## Repayment

If the employee leaves Quillmoor voluntarily:

- within **12 months** of the move: **100%** of the lump sum must be repaid;
- within **24 months** of the move: **50%** must be repaid.

## Approval

Relocations are approved by the department head and the Director of People & Culture.
