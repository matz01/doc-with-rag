# Hiring Process Guide

*Owner: People & Culture · For hiring managers*

## Before opening a role

Every role must be included in the approved 2026 headcount plan. Roles outside the plan need approval from the CFO and the Director of People & Culture.

## Interview stages

1. **Recruiter screen** — 30 minutes
2. **Hiring manager interview** — 45 minutes
3. **Role exercise** — a take-home exercise of **maximum 4 hours**, or a 90-minute live exercise
4. **Team interview** — 60 minutes with two future colleagues
5. **Values interview** — 30 minutes with a member of the leadership team

## Timelines

- Target time from first interview to offer: **21 days**.
- Give the candidate a decision **within 48 hours** of the final interview.

## Offers

- Offers above the band **midpoint** need approval from the Director of People & Culture (Sofia Brandt).
- Offers above the band **maximum** need approval from the CEO.
- References are required for roles at **L4 and above**.

## Employee referrals

Employees who refer a successful candidate receive a **€1,500 referral bonus**, paid once the new hire passes the **6-month probation period**.
