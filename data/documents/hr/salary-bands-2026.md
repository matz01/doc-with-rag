# Salary Bands 2026

*Owner: People & Culture · CONFIDENTIAL — People & Culture and leadership only · Effective from 1 January 2026*

All figures are gross annual base salary in EUR for **Milan**, full-time.

## General bands (all departments except Engineering)

| Level | Title examples | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Junior / Associate | €30,000 | €33,000 | €36,000 |
| L2 | Mid-level / Specialist | €36,000 | €40,500 | €45,000 |
| L3 | Senior | €45,000 | €51,500 | €58,000 |
| L4 | Lead / Manager | €58,000 | €65,000 | €72,000 |
| L5 | Principal / Head of | €72,000 | €81,000 | €90,000 |

## Engineering bands

| L3 | Senior Engineer | €49,000 | €56,000 | €63,000 |
| L4 | Staff Engineer / Engineering Manager | €63,000 | €70,500 | €78,000 |
| L5 | Principal Engineer / Head of Engineering | €78,000 | €87,500 | €97,000 |

## Lisbon

Lisbon bands are **85% of the Milan figures**, rounded to the nearest €500.

## Sales roles

Sales roles have an on-target earnings (OTE) package split **60% base / 40% variable**. The base salary follows the general bands. The variable part follows the Sales Commission Plan.

## Rules

- Offers above the **midpoint** need approval from the Director of People & Culture.
- Offers above the **maximum** need approval from the CEO.
- Bands are reviewed every year in December.
