# Performance Review Process

*Owner: People & Culture · For people managers*

## Cycles

There are two review cycles per year:

- **Mid-year review** — September
- **Year-end review** — March

Each cycle includes a self-review, a manager review, peer feedback (at least 2 peers) and a calibration meeting per department.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 2 | Partially meets expectations |
| 1 | Below expectations |

## Merit increases

The **2026 merit increase budget is 3.5% of total payroll**. Guidelines per rating:

| Rating | Merit increase |
|---|---|
| 5 | 6–8% |
| 4 | 4–5% |
| 3 | 2–3% |
| 1–2 | 0% |

Merit increases are applied with the April payroll.

## Promotions

Promotions are effective on **1 April** or **1 October**. A promotion usually comes with a salary increase of **8–12%**, within the new level's band.

## Performance improvement plans

An employee rated 1 receives a **60-day performance improvement plan** with clear goals and fortnightly check-ins with their manager and an HR Business Partner.
