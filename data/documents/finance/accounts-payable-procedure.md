# Accounts Payable Procedure

*Owner: Finance*

## Receiving invoices

All supplier invoices are received and processed in **Ledgerline**, our own product. Invoices sent to individual employees must be forwarded to **ap@quillmoor.example**.

## Purchase orders

A purchase order (PO) is required for any purchase above **€2,000**. Invoices above €2,000 without a matching PO are put on hold.

## Approval matrix

| Invoice amount | Approvers |
|---|---|
| up to €5,000 | Budget owner (department head) |
| €5,000 – €25,000 | Budget owner + Finance Controller |
| above €25,000 | Budget owner + Finance Controller + CFO |
| above €100,000 | All of the above + CEO |

## Payment terms and runs

- Standard payment terms are **net 30 days** from the invoice date, unless the contract says otherwise.
- Payment runs take place every **Tuesday and Friday**.
- Urgent same-day payments must be requested **before 12:00** and approved by the Finance Controller.

## Supplier bank details

- Bank details of new suppliers are verified with a **phone call-back** to a number from a trusted source, never from the invoice or email.
- Any change of supplier bank details requires approval from **two members** of the Finance team.
