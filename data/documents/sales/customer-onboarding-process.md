# Customer Onboarding Process

*Owner: Customer Success · For Sales and Engineering*

## Handover

The Account Executive hands the customer over to Customer Success at the **kickoff meeting**, which must take place **within 5 business days of contract signature**. Enterprise customers get a named Customer Success Manager.

## Implementation timelines

| Plan | Standard implementation |
|---|---|
| Starter | 2 weeks |
| Growth | 4 weeks |
| Enterprise | 6–10 weeks |

## Phases

1. **Kickoff** — goals, contacts, project plan
2. **ERP integration** — connection and field mapping
3. **Data migration** — suppliers, open invoices, approval rules
4. **User acceptance testing (UAT)**
5. **Go-live**
6. **Hypercare** — **30 days** of priority support after go-live

## Supported ERPs

Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle NetSuite and TeamSystem have native connectors. SAP S/4HANA is currently integrated through file exchange; a native connector is on the product roadmap.

## Escalations

Technical blockers during implementation are escalated to the Engineering on-call team only for production incidents. All other questions go through the Customer Success Slack channel #cs-implementation.
