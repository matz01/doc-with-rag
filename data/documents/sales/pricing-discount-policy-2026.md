# Pricing and Discount Policy — 2026

*Owner: Sales & Finance · CONFIDENTIAL · Effective from 1 January 2026 · Replaces the 2025 policy*

## List prices (per month, billed annually)

| Plan | Price | Included volume |
|---|---|---|
| Ledgerline Starter | €900 | up to 1,000 invoices per month |
| Ledgerline Growth | €2,400 | up to 5,000 invoices per month |
| Ledgerline Enterprise | from €6,500 | custom volume, SSO, dedicated Customer Success Manager |

**Ledgerline Insights** add-on: **20%** of the base subscription price.

Monthly billing is available with a **10% surcharge**.

## Implementation fees (one-off)

| Plan | Fee |
|---|---|
| Starter | none |
| Growth | €3,500 |
| Enterprise | €12,000 |

Implementation fees can be waived only by the Sales Director.

## Discount authority

Discount limits apply to the **total discount** on the subscription, including any multi-year discount.

| Role | Maximum total discount |
|---|---|
| Account Executive | 10% |
| Sales Director | 20% |
| CFO (Marta Kowalczyk) | above 20%, up to the absolute maximum of 30% |

## Multi-year contracts

A **3-year contract with annual prepayment** receives a standard **8% discount**. This counts toward the discount authority limits above.
