# Competitor Battlecard

*Owner: Sales & Marketing · Internal use only*

## Payvanta

- **Positioning:** low-cost AP automation for small and mid-size companies.
- **Strengths:** list prices about **15–20% lower** than Ledgerline; fast self-service setup.
- **Weaknesses:** limited ERP integrations (no Microsoft Dynamics 365 connector); no native Italian e-invoicing (SDI) support; approval workflows cannot handle multiple legal entities.
- **How to win:** focus on total cost of ownership and multi-entity workflows. Show the SDI integration live.

## InvoiceHub Pro

- **Positioning:** enterprise AP suite, strong in the UK.
- **Strengths:** large partner network; mature reporting.
- **Weaknesses:** customer data hosted **in the US only**; implementations typically take 4–6 months; weak support in Italian and Portuguese.
- **How to win:** lead with **EU-only data hosting**, faster implementation (6 weeks for Enterprise) and local-language support.

## Ledgerline differentiators

1. All customer data hosted in the EU.
2. Native Italian SDI e-invoicing and Portuguese invoicing compliance.
3. Multi-entity approval workflows.
4. Customers reduce invoice approval time by **3.2 days on average**.

## Common objections

- *"Payvanta is cheaper."* — Compare the cost of manual work on SDI invoices and multi-entity approvals, not only the licence.
- *"We already have an ERP module."* — Show the approval workflow and the extraction accuracy on the prospect's own invoices.
