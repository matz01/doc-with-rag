# Sales Commission Plan — 2026

*Owner: Sales, Finance and People & Culture · CONFIDENTIAL · Effective 1 January – 31 December 2026*

## Who is eligible

Account Executives and the Sales Director. On-target earnings are split 60% base salary and 40% variable.

## Commission rates

| Deal type | Commission |
|---|---|
| New business | **10%** of first-year ACV |
| Upsell / expansion | **8%** of the incremental ACV |
| Renewal | **4%** of the renewed ACV |

ACV (annual contract value) **excludes implementation fees**. For multi-year contracts, commission is paid on the **first-year ACV only**.

## Quota and accelerator

- Annual quota for Account Executives: **€600,000** of new-business ACV.
- Above 100% of annual quota, commission is paid at **1.5×** the standard rate.

## Payment

Commission is paid **quarterly**, with the payroll of the month after the quarter closes.

## Clawback

If a customer churns or does not pay within **6 months** of signature, **100%** of the related commission is clawed back from future payments.
