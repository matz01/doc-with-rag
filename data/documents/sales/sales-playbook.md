# Sales Playbook

*Owner: Sales · For the sales team*

## Ideal customer profile

- Mid-market companies with **200 to 2,000 employees**
- Processing **more than 1,500 supplier invoices per month**
- Core industries: logistics, retail, healthcare, professional services

## Sales stages

1. Discovery
2. Demo
3. Technical validation
4. Proposal
5. Negotiation
6. Closed won / Closed lost

We qualify opportunities with **MEDDICC**. An opportunity cannot move to Proposal without an identified economic buyer.

## CRM hygiene

- Every open opportunity must have a next step and a next-step date.
- Opportunities with **no activity for 21 days** are flagged in the weekly pipeline meeting.

## Trials and pilots

We do **not** offer free trials. Enterprise prospects can run a **30-day pilot** with a signed pilot agreement. Use **demo.ledgerline.example** for demos.
