# On-Call Policy

*Owner: Engineering*

## Rotation

- On-call shifts last **one week**, starting **Monday at 10:00 CET**.
- Nobody is on call more than **one week in every four**.
- Swaps are arranged directly between engineers and recorded in **Pagerline**.
- Engineers on annual leave are never scheduled for on-call.

## Expectations

- Acknowledge pages within **15 minutes**.
- Be able to reach your laptop and a stable internet connection within **30 minutes** at any time during your shift.

## Compensation

- **On-call allowance: €250 per week** of on-call.
- **Call-out payment: €60 per incident** handled outside working hours.
- **Time off in lieu** equal to the time spent on incidents outside working hours, with a minimum of 1 hour per call-out. Time off in lieu must be used within 2 months.

## Eligibility

Engineers join the rotation after 3 months, as described in Engineering Onboarding.
