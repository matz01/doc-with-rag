# Employee Handbook

*Owner: People & Culture · Applies to all employees in Milan and Lisbon*

## 1. Working hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Everyone is expected to be available during **core hours, 10:00–16:00 CET** (09:00–15:00 local time for the Lisbon office). Outside core hours you can organise your day freely, in agreement with your team.

Remote work rules are described in the Remote Work Policy.

## 2. Probation and notice periods

- New employees have a **probation period of 6 months**.
- During probation, either party can end the contract with **2 weeks' notice**.
- After probation, the notice period is **1 month** for levels L1–L3 and **2 months** for levels L4–L5.

## 3. Absence and sick leave

- If you are sick, notify your manager **before 10:00** on the first day of absence.
- Register the absence in **Peoplebase**, our HR system.
- A medical certificate is required for absences longer than **2 consecutive days**.

Annual leave is covered by the Annual Leave Policy.

## 4. Code of conduct

- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are never tolerated.
- You may accept gifts or hospitality from suppliers or customers only up to a value of **€75**. Anything above must be declined or reported.
- Report concerns about conduct to your manager, to People & Culture, or confidentially to **compliance@quillmoor.example**.

## 5. Internal tools

| Need | Tool |

## 6. Confidentiality

Customer data, financial results, salaries and contracts are confidential. Share them only with people who need them for their job.
