# Annual Leave Policy — 2025

*Owner: People & Culture · Effective from 1 January 2025*

## Entitlement

- Full-time employees receive **25 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement.

## Requesting leave

- Request leave in Peoplebase.
- For more than 3 consecutive days, submit the request **at least 5 working days in advance**.

## Carry-over

You can carry over **up to 3 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used **by 30 June 2026**.

## Year-end company closure

The company is closed from 24 December 2025 to 1 January 2026. **Two** of the closure days are deducted from your annual leave.
