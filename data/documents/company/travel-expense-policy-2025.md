# Travel and Expense Policy — 2025

*Owner: Finance · Effective from 1 January 2025*

## Approval

Business trips must be approved by your manager before booking.

## Transport

- Flights: economy class.
- Trains: second class.
- Use of a private car: reimbursed at **€0.38 per km**.

## Accommodation

- Standard hotel cap: **€140 per night**.
- London, Paris and Zurich: **€180 per night**.

## Meals

- Domestic trips: **€40 per day**.
- International trips: **€55 per day**.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in Spendly **within 60 days**. Receipts are required for any expense above **€20**.
