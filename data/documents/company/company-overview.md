# Quillmoor Software — Company Overview

*Owner: People & Culture · Last reviewed: July 2026*

## Who we are

Quillmoor Software builds **Ledgerline**, a cloud platform that automates accounts payable for mid-market companies: invoice capture, approval workflows, ERP synchronisation and payment preparation. Our add-on product, **Ledgerline Insights**, gives finance teams spend analytics and supplier dashboards.

Quillmoor was founded in **2014 in Milan** by Frank Adeyemi and Tomás Ferreira. The legal entity is Quillmoor Software S.r.l.

## Mission and values

Our mission is to give finance teams their time back. Our values:

1. **Own the outcome** — we finish what we start and fix what we break.
2. **Make it simple** — for customers and for each other.
3. **Say it straight** — honest, respectful and direct feedback.
4. **Learn in public** — share what worked and what did not.
