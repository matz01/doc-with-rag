# Remote Work Policy — 2025

*Owner: People & Culture · Effective from 1 January 2025*

## Hybrid schedule

- Employees can work remotely **up to 2 days per week**.
- **Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days**: everyone works from their office on those days.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for **up to 10 working days per calendar year**, with approval from People & Culture.

## Fully remote arrangements

Fully remote work is not available, except for temporary situations agreed with People & Culture.

## Home office support

- **Home office allowance: €300**, one-off, for new joiners.
- **Internet allowance: €20 per month**, paid through payroll.

## Expectations

When working remotely you must be reachable during core hours and use a secure connection.
