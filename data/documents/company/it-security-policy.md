# IT Security Policy

*Owner: Engineering — Security · Applies to all employees and contractors*

## Accounts and passwords

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long and unique for each service.
- Use **Keyvault**, the company password manager, for all work credentials.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory** on every company account.

## Devices

- Company laptops have full-disk encryption enabled. Do not disable it.
- Screens must lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity.
- **USB storage devices are blocked** on company laptops.
- Personal devices may access company email only through the managed mobile app. They must not store customer data.

## Reporting incidents

Report any suspected security incident, phishing email you clicked, or lost or stolen device **within 1 hour** to **security@quillmoor.example** and in the **#security-help** channel. Reporting quickly matters more than knowing exactly what happened.

## Phishing and training

- Phishing simulations run every quarter.
- Everyone must complete the annual security training **by 31 March** each year.

## Customer data

Customer invoice data must never be copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage or public AI tools.
