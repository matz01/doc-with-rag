# Remote Work Policy — 2026

*Owner: People & Culture · Effective from 1 January 2026 · Replaces the Remote Work Policy 2025*

## Hybrid schedule

- Employees can work remotely **up to 3 days per week**.
- **Tuesday is the anchor day**: everyone works from their office on Tuesdays, unless on leave or travelling for work.
- Teams can agree additional in-office days for planning or workshops, announced at least one week in advance.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for **up to 20 working days per calendar year**. Requests must be approved by People & Culture **at least 15 days in advance**, because of tax and insurance checks.

## Fully remote arrangements

Fully remote work is an exception. It requires approval from both your manager and the **Director of People & Culture**, and is reviewed every 12 months.

## Home office support

- **Home office allowance: €400**, one-off, to buy a desk, chair or other equipment. It is available to new joiners and again every 4 years.
- **Internet allowance: €30 per month**, paid through payroll.

Claim the home office allowance in Spendly with receipts within 90 days of purchase.

## Expectations

When working remotely you must be reachable during core hours, use a secure connection, and follow the IT Security Policy.
