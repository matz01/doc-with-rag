# Annual Leave Policy — 2026

*Owner: People & Culture · Effective from 1 January 2026 · Replaces the Annual Leave Policy 2025*

## Entitlement

- Full-time employees receive **26 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement.
- After **5 years of service**, employees receive **1 additional day** (27 days in total).
- Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave (see the 2026 Holiday Calendar).

## Requesting leave

- Request leave in **Peoplebase**.
- For more than 3 consecutive days, submit the request **at least 10 working days in advance**.
- Your manager should reply within 3 working days.

## Carry-over

You can carry over **up to 5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used **by 31 March 2027**. Any other unused days are forfeited, unless People & Culture agreed an exception in writing.

## Year-end company closure

The company is closed from **24 December 2026 to 1 January 2027**. Three of the closure days — **28, 29 and 30 December** — are deducted from your annual leave. The other closure days are public holidays or company-paid days.

## Leave and on-call

Employees on annual leave cannot be scheduled for on-call duty.
