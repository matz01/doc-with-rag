# Learning and Development Policy

*Owner: People & Culture*

## Budget

Every employee has a learning budget of **€1,200 per calendar year**. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

The budget covers courses, books, online subscriptions, certification exams and conference tickets. Travel to conferences is covered by the department travel budget, following the Travel and Expense Policy.

## Learning days

Every employee can take **5 learning days per year** to attend courses or conferences. Learning days are not deducted from annual leave.

## Approval

Ask your manager for approval in Peoplebase before you book. Your manager should reply within 5 working days.

## Exceptional requests

Courses or certifications costing more than **€2,000** need approval from both your manager and People & Culture. If you leave Quillmoor within **12 months** of completing such a course, you must repay **50%** of its cost.

## Sharing what you learn

After a conference or a major course, share a short summary with your team or in the monthly "Learn in public" session.
