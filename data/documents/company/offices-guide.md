# Offices Guide

*Owner: Workplace team*

## Addresses

- **Milan (headquarters):** Viale Ardenza 21, 20143 Milan, Italy
- **Lisbon:** Rua Nova do Tejo 140, 1200-610 Lisbon, Portugal

## Opening hours and access

Both offices are open **Monday to Friday, 07:30–20:00**. Access is by personal badge. Lost badges must be reported to reception; a replacement costs nothing the first time.

## Meeting rooms

- Milan has **9 meeting rooms**, Lisbon has **5**.
- Book rooms in **Roomly**. Large rooms (8+ seats) can be booked for a maximum of **2 hours** at a time.

## Guests

Hosts must register guests in Roomly **by 17:00 on the day before** the visit. Guests receive a temporary badge at reception and must be accompanied at all times.

## Wi-Fi

- **Quillmoor-Staff**: employees, login with your company account (SSO).
- **Quillmoor-Guest**: visitors, daily password available at reception.

## Parking and bikes

- Milan has **12 parking spaces**, assigned through a monthly draw in Roomly.
- Lisbon has no car parking. Both offices have secure bike storage.

## Food

- Milan has no canteen; employees in Italy receive meal vouchers (see the Benefits Overview).
- Lisbon has a shared kitchen with free coffee and fruit.
