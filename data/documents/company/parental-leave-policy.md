# Parental Leave Policy

*Owner: People & Culture · Applies to employees in Italy and Portugal*

Quillmoor complements the statutory parental leave available in each country.

## Birth parents and primary caregivers

Quillmoor tops up statutory parental pay to **100% of salary for the first 16 weeks** of leave.

## Non-birth parents and secondary caregivers

Quillmoor provides **4 weeks of leave at 100% pay**, in addition to the statutory leave.

## Adoption

Adoptive parents are entitled to the same benefits as birth and non-birth parents, from the date the child joins the family.

## Notice

Tell People & Culture **at least 8 weeks before** your expected leave start date, so that we can plan cover and payroll.

## Returning to work

On return, you can work a **phased return of 4 weeks at 80% of your hours on full pay**.
